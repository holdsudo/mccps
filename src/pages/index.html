FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious?

How does the free savings analysis work?

Tell us a little about your business and send two months of processing statements. We review them line by line, show you exactly what you’re paying and where the money goes, and give you a no-obligation proposal.

Can I keep my current terminal?

Often, yes. Many terminals can be re-programmed for MCCPS, so you don’t have to buy new hardware. If you need something new, we offer countertop, wireless and mobile options with EMV chip and tap to pay.

What is the Zero Processing Fees program?

It’s a compliant dual-pricing program: you display a cash price and a card price, so card processing costs are covered at checkout — which can bring your processing cost to $0. Card-network rules and state laws apply, and we help you set it up with proper signage and receipts.

How fast do I get my money?

Next-day funding is available, so deposits can land the next business day after you batch out.

Do you support online and recurring payments?

Yes — a secure online gateway for your website or cart, a virtual terminal for phone orders, invoices and payment links, and recurring billing for subscriptions and memberships.

Is support really 24/7?

Yes. Free 24/7 technical support and personal, first-hand customer service — call 844.826.6227 any time.
