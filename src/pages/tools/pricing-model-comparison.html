Why the model matters

Flat-rate pricing is simple but bundles everything into one number. Tiered pricing sorts transactions into buckets the processor defines — the more that land in “non-qualified”, the more you pay. Interchange-plus passes card-network costs through at cost and shows the processor’s markup separately, which makes it the easiest to audit.
