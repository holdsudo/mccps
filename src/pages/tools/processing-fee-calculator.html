Effective rate = total fees ÷ total card volume. Ranges labelled here are general rules of thumb; what’s competitive depends on your card mix, ticket size and how you accept payments. Savings shown are hypothetical.

Why effective rate beats the advertised rate

Processors quote rates many ways — flat, tiered, interchange-plus — and add monthly, PCI, batch and statement fees on top. Dividing total fees by total volume cuts through all of it.
