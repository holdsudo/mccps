How this estimate works

Your effective rate is total processing fees divided by total card volume. Multiply your monthly volume by that rate and by twelve, and you have your annual cost of accepting cards. Lowering the processor’s markup changes that number; a compliant Zero Processing Fees program can move it toward $0.
