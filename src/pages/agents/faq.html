M.C.C.P.S. independent agent F.A.Q.s

Everything you need to know about referring merchants, building a team and getting paid.

Why become an M.C.C.P.S. agent?

So you can help your merchants reduce their credit card rates and fees, work on your own time, and grow your income on your own terms.

How do I make money with M.C.C.P.S.?

Very simple: you refer merchants who are willing to let us analyze their credit card statements. We work to reduce their rates and fees, and you continuously get paid for as long as the referred merchant processes through MCCPS.

Can I build my own sales team?

Yes. You receive overrides and commissions on your down-line sales team’s referrals, for as long as they are referred.

Do you have training available?

Yes. Once you’re set up as a sales agent, one of our MCCPS team members will meet with you to get you fully set up with sales and marketing materials.

Who are my potential clients?

Any business that accepts credit cards as a form of payment.

How do I get them to sign up?

All you need to do is have them give you a copy of their credit card processing statement for our office to analyze for rate and fee reduction.

Can they use their own terminals?

They can use their existing terminals if they own them and if they are re-programmable.

Do I get reports on my merchants?

Yes. You will be able to view all reporting for all of your merchants.

How do I get paid, and where do I sign?

You receive continuous monthly residuals against your referred merchants’ credit card processing. Contact our H/R rep at 844.826.6227 to get your agreement started.
